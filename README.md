## 🏭 About the Company

Flourish Ventures Limited operates on advanced **STOLL computerized flat-knitting technology**[cite: 13], supplying export-oriented garment manufacturers and global apparel brands with premium-quality knitwear production capacity[cite: 10].

## 🛠️️ Manufacturing Infrastructure & Capacity

The facility integrates an end-to-end production workflow structured across specialized sections:
1. **Winding Section:** Efficient yarn preparation featuring auto-winding and part-opener machines[cite: 13].
2. **Knitting Section:** STOLL computerized flat-knitting machines covering a wide gauge range (3 GG to 16 GG)[cite: 13].
3. **Linking Section:** Dial-linking machines configured for precise garment panel assembly[cite: 13].
4. **Finishing & Assembly:** Hydro extractors, heavy-duty drying machines, vacuum steam irons, JUKI sewing/overlock machines, automated button/bartack equipment, and needle detectors[cite: 14].
5. **Utility & Power Support:** Reliable industrial steam boilers (gas and electric) coupled with heavy-duty diesel generators to ensure uninterrupted operations[cite: 14].
