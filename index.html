About Us

A specialized sweater-manufacturing partner within Bangladesh's export-oriented RMG ecosystem.

Flourish Ventures Limited is a 100% export-oriented sweater manufacturing factory, based in Gazipur on the outskirts of Dhaka. The company runs on STOLL flat-knitting machines, supplying export-oriented garment manufacturers with high-quality knitwear production capacity.

Built around disciplined operations, a structured management system, and continued investment in modern technology, Flourish Ventures is positioned as a dependable production partner within Bangladesh's ready-made garment export ecosystem.
